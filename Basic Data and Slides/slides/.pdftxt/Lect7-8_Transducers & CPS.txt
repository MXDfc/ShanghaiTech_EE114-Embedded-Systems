   What is a sensor? An actuator?
   • A sensor is a device that measures a physical quantity
   → Input / “Read from physical world”
   • An actuator is a device that modifies a physical quantity
   → Output / “Write to physical world”

   Sensor specifications                                                                            模电知识 ！
   • Calibration
      – Relating measurements to the physical phenomenon
   • Range
      – Max. & Min. values for input or output
   • Sensitivity
      – Slope, dB/dA is linearized output/input
   • Nonlinearity
      – Measurements may not be proportional to physical
        phenomenon
      – Feedback can be used to keep operating point in the
        linear region
   • Sampling
      – Aliasing, Missed events
   • Noise
      – Analog signal conditioning
      – Filtering & latency
   • Frequency response
      – The bandwidth

   Actuators - Stepper motor                                                       步进电机是一种可以将脉冲信号转换
                                                                                   为角位移或线位移的开环控制电机。
   • Stepper motor
                                                                              1. 直流无刷电机比步进电机的转速高。
                                                                              2. 直流无刷电机与步进电机的驱动原理不同，
                                                                                 直流无刷电机是靠霍尔元件定位来提供交变电源
                                                                                 控制转动的。步进电机是靠单项脉冲电压直接驱
                                                                                 动的，不需要霍尔元件定位，可以通过控制加给
   • More poles                                                                  电机的脉冲个数，来精确定位旋转的角度。
                                                                              3. 一般直流无刷电机用于控制精度要求不高的地方。
                                                                                 步进电机就用于控制精度要求比较高的地方。

                                                                                               1) 使用GPIO 延时模拟脉冲控制
                                                                                               2) 使用GPIO 中断模拟脉冲控制
                                                                                               3) 使用PWM 比较输出
                                                                                               4) 使用PWM 控制匀速旋转

   系统设计
   • 基于单片机的电子称系统主要分为六个部分：
      – 第一部分：称重传感器。用来感应被测重力，输出微弱的毫伏级电压信号 (压力->电压)；后续通过
        专用ADC芯片进行调理转换；选型比较后选用一款4线全桥称重传感器。
      – 第二部分：24位A/D芯片HX711电路。用于对传感器信号进行调理转换。HX711 是海芯科技一款专
        为高精度电子秤而设计的24 位A/D 转换器芯片，内置增益控制，精度高，性能稳定。
        HX711芯片通过2线串行方式(I2C) 与单片机通信。
      – 第三部分：单片机STC89C52为控制核心，实现电子秤基本功能 。
      – 第四部分：按键输入。设置称重上限，控制等功能。
      – 第五部分：电源系统。为单片机、HX711电路以及传感器供电。
      – 第六部分：液晶显示。显示物体的重量，以及电子秤量程。

     出反映脉搏跳动的电压信号。采样时间由定时器确定。

   Modeling, Design, Analysis
    Modeling is the process of
    gaining a deeper understanding
    of a system through imitation.
    Models express what a system does
    or should do.
    Design is the structured creation of artifacts.
    It specifies how a system does what it does.
    Analysis is the process of gaining a deeper
    understanding of a system through dissection.
    It specifies why a system does what it does
    (or fails to do what a model says it should do).
                                                                                                                                 (Lee & Seshia @ Berkeley)

   Finite State Machines (FSM)
   • or Finite State Automation (FSA)
      – Models of behaviors of a system or object, with a limited number of defined conditions or
        modes, where modes change with circumstance

   • 4 main elements:
      – States: define behavior and may produce actions
      – State transitions: move from one state to another
      – Rules (or conditions): must be met to allow a state transition
      – Input events: externally or internally generated; may trigger rules and lead to state
        transitions

   • An initial state and a current state
                              Good for representation and
                              modeling sequences of behaviors
